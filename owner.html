    STEP 4 · SWAP ON BASE
    ETH ↔ USDC
    Quotes come directly from Uniswap v3 pools. FYNX charges no service fee for these tests. Pool fees and blockchain fees apply. Quotes expire after one minute.

    Review contract operation

    STEP 5 · TRACK
    Track confirmation.
    Transactions you submit from this page appear below. “Check status” reads the network and tells you if one is waiting, confirmed (with a confirmations count), failed, replaced by a sped-up or cancelled version, dropped, or moved by a block reorganisation. Checking never signs or resends anything. This is not a complete wallet history: use the block explorer for older activity.
      
          
      CARDS & BANKS · SANDBOX
      Prepare buying and selling.
      FYNX will use a regulated payment provider for card and bank checkout. A Transak sandbox adapter is prepared; it needs your partner account and approved connection before testing. No real card or bank payments are enabled.
      Open Transak sandbox ↗
      Use only the provider's test credentials in its sandbox. Payment and KYC information stays with the provider. Opening or returning from checkout never confirms payment. Transak staging ETH buys do not deliver ETH; use this to test the checkout only.
      
          
      REAL-MONEY SENDING · BASE
      Your switch, your limits.
      Off · Base is read-only
      When on, Crypto tools can prepare ETH and USDC transfers on Base up to your limits, with the same review, simulation and checks as on the test networks. Your wallet still asks you to approve every transfer. Swaps with real money stay off. Turn this on only after the security, legal and pilot gates in Launch readiness are done.
      Needs a sign-in within the last 5 minutes. Every change is written to the security audit log. Turning off takes effect at once, also for a transfer you were reviewing.
