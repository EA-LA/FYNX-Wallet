    PASSKEYS
    A simpler way back.
    Use your device’s biometric or PIN verification to sign in to FYNX. A FYNX passkey protects account access; your connected wallet still approves each signature.
    Sign in again if your login is more than five minutes old. Keep Google or email access as a backup.
    ACCOUNT & RECOVERY
    Keep access in your hands.
    • Sign-in stays in memory and ends when this page closes or after 15 minutes of inactivity.
    • Changing the wallet account or network clears the current signing and transfer review.
    • Never enter a seed phrase or private key on this website or send one to support.
    • Recover a wallet using the wallet provider’s official recovery process. FYNX cannot reset wallet keys.
